# Albert Kho

I study finance and build tools to explore questions from class. My research uses daily market data. My web projects range from an interactive money market to multilingual sites for clients.

## Research

**Is a Daily Return a Coin Flip?** I test five assumptions behind that analogy. For Bitcoin, I look at the frequency of up days, the time between extreme moves, and the shape of the return distribution. For Chinese A-shares, I examine what daily price limits do to the distribution. [Research repository](https://github.com/albertkho528/return-distribution-tests)

**Risk and Return Predictability in Bitcoin**. We tested momentum and volatility forecasts on 2024–2025 data after choosing the models on earlier years. The momentum forecast fell short of the historical mean. Exponential weighting improved the volatility forecast, but the volatility-targeted strategy still had a lower net Sharpe ratio than the fixed Bitcoin and Treasury-bill mix. [Notebooks](https://github.com/albertkho528/btc-risk-return-predictability) · [Interactive code explorer](https://btc-risk-return-predictability.vercel.app)
